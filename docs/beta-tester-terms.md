# Cargo beta tester terms — draft for legal review

**Status:** DRAFT, not yet reviewed by a solicitor. Version `2026-10-17`.
The app shows this text (from `src/lib/security/betaTerms.js`) to every
tester on a TestFlight build and records who accepted which version, when and
on what device (`terms_acceptances`). Change both together and bump
`BETA_TERMS_VERSION` so everyone re-accepts.

Points for the solicitor to check:
- Entity name: "Cargo Technology". Use the registered company name and number.
- Governing law: England and Wales assumed.
- Whether click-through acceptance is enough, or testers outside the vessel
  (e.g. management companies) should sign a separate NDA.
- The monitoring paragraph against UK GDPR transparency. The Privacy Policy
  should also mention sign-in, screenshot and screen-recording logging.
- How long confidentiality should last, and any liability cap.

Technology ("we", "us"). Please read these terms. By tapping "I agree" you
accept them for as long as you use the beta.

1.2 You must not show, describe, share, publish or post the beta or any part

permission.

2.1 All rights in Cargo, including its software, design, look and feel,

2.2 You must not copy, reproduce, adapt, reverse engineer or decompile Cargo,

### 3. Your account is for you only

else use your account, or sign in for someone else.
3.2 We may limit how many devices can be signed in to one account at a time
and sign out older devices.

### 4. Monitoring
4.1 To protect the beta, the app records sign-ins (time, device and
approximate location from your IP address) and when a screenshot or screen
recording is taken in the app. Your vessel's Command team and Cargo
Technology can see this activity.

5.1 We may use any ideas, suggestions and feedback you give us about Cargo
freely, without payment or attribution, and they become part of Cargo.

so report anything that looks wrong. The beta is provided "as is", and to the
extent the law allows we are not liable for losses arising from its use.
6.2 Your vessel's data remains your vessel's. These terms do not change how
it is owned or handled under the Cargo Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

### 7. Ending access
7.1 We may suspend or end your access to the beta at any time, including if
we believe these terms have been broken. If you break these terms we may take
legal action, including to stop further disclosure or copying.

### 8. Law
8.1 These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the courts
of England and Wales have jurisdiction.
